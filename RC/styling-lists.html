Recipe List

This is a list of all the steps needed to make a delicious pitta.

  1. Toast pitta, leave to cool, then slice down the edge.
  2. Fry the chicken in a shallow, non-stick pan, until cooked through.
  3. Wash and chop the salad.
  4. Fill pitta with salad, greek yoghurt, humous, and fried chicken.
